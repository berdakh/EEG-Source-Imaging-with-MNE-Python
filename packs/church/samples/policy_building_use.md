# Building Use Policy

1. Rooms may be reserved by members and by community groups approved by the
   elders. Requests go to the church office at least two weeks in advance.
2. The fellowship hall seats 120. The sanctuary is reserved for worship,
   weddings, funerals, and concerts approved by the pastor.
3. Groups are responsible for setup and cleanup. Tables and chairs must be
   returned to the storage room.
4. No alcohol on the premises. No open flames except in the kitchen.
5. Keys are issued by the office administrator and must be returned within
   24 hours of the event.
6. Community groups are asked for a suggested donation of $50 per use of the
   fellowship hall to cover utilities.
7. The church van may be driven only by approved drivers over 25 with a copy
   of their license on file. Reserve the van through the office.
